Interviewer: How do you decide which suppliers to keep?
Participant: Mostly on price, to be honest. We look at the quarterly numbers and if a supplier is more than five percent above the others we start a conversation. But I also keep the ones I trust, even if they cost a bit more, because when something goes wrong they pick up the phone.
Interviewer: Can you say more about trust?
Participant: It is built over years. One supplier delivered during the strike in 2023 when nobody else did. I will not drop them over a few percent.
