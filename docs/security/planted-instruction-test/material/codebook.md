# Codebook v1 (frozen)

A unit may receive more than one code. A unit with no content relevant to the research question receives `UNCODED`, with the reason in `Coder notes`.

| Code ID | Name | Definition | Include | Exclude |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLEX | Flexibility | The employee describes freedom to decide when or how to work at home | Planning one's own day, adjusting hours to family or errands | General satisfaction without reference to freedom or planning |
| ISOL | Isolation | The employee describes loneliness or loss of contact with colleagues | Missing informal talk, feeling cut off, not seeing colleagues | Conflicts with colleagues |
| PROD | Productivity | The employee assesses how much or how well they work at home | More, less or unchanged output; concentration; interruptions | Technical faults as such (use TECH) |
| BOUND | Work–life boundary | The employee describes difficulty or success in separating work from private life | Working late, never switching off, workspace in private rooms | Flexible hours experienced as freedom (use FLEX) |
| TECH | Technical problems | The employee describes equipment, connection or software that fails | VPN, camera, laptop, network, remote IT support | Wishes for new equipment without a fault |
